Getting started

Two ways in, depending on whether you want to adjust anything.

If you wantDo this
A ready-to-print fileGrab an STL from library/ or from the GitHub Releases page. No software needed beyond a slicer.
To change the dimensionsOpen cable-box-parametric.scad in OpenSCAD, press F3 for the Customizer, adjust, then F6 to render and export.
To start from a presetLoad a config.json from library/ in the Customizer's preset dropdown, then tweak.
Dimensions are outer dimensions. Box_Width, Box_Depth, and Box_Height describe the outside of the shell. Usable interior is smaller by Wall_Thickness on each side, and smaller again wherever stabilizer fins sit.
